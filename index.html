Live Trade Record

A signal is on the bench only if it passes all nine: at least 8 real trades tested • profit factor ≥ 1.5 • Sharpe ≥ 0.3 • profit target ≥ 5% (no upper limit) • exits within 30 trading days • statistically real in at least one half of its own price history • no single trade over 40% of the signal’s total gains • the weaker half of history not measurably edgeless • a live win rate of 90%+ on 8–11 recorded trades or 80%+ on 12+ (added 2026-09-19).
